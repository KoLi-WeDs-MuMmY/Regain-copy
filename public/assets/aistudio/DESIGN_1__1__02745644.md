## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of analog instrument craftsmanship and high-fidelity digital mindfulness. Built for knowledge workers, creators, and professionals seeking intentional focus, it merges tactile mechanical warmth with clean, modern digital utility.

The aesthetic philosophy draws from:
- **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Precision**: Physical rotary dials, engraved notch tracks, weighted toggles, and soft debossed card containers that feel calibrated rather than purely flat.
- **Warm Minimalist Architecture**: Deep charcoal canvases paired with burnt vermilion, terracotta, soft ivory, and gentle ceramic tones. It rejects sterile cold minimalism in favor of rich, grounding, room-temperature surfaces.
- **Sculptural Bento Modularity**: Information is organized into asymmetric, rounded-3xl modules and nested capsules that provide immediate focus without visual strain.

## Colors

The palette establishes an introspective, deeply focused environment anchored by low-glare organic darks and radiant warm accents:

- **Primary (`#E75A44` — Burnt Vermilion / Terracotta)**: Applied to high-intent active states, live countdown ticks, primary action pills, and focus gauge indicators.
- **Secondary (`#5D6BFA` — Cobalt / Indigo Hue)**: Used for secondary cognitive modes, deep work tagging, and comparative analytics accents.
- **Tertiary (`#EADECF` — Warm Cream Ivory)**: Provides high-readability analog dial backgrounds, clock face discs, and crisp physical counter backdrops.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#131416` — Deep Obsidian Charcoal)**: Formulates the dark ground layer with subtle mineral depth, avoiding pure harsh black (`#000000`).

Supporting structural tokens include:
- `surface-elevated`: `#1C1E22` for bento tiles.
- `surface-inset`: `#0D0E10` for sunken track recesses and notch wells.
- `text-primary`: `#F6F6F7`
- `text-muted`: `#7E838D`
- `accent-glow`: `rgba(231, 90, 68, 0.16)`

## Typography

Typography prioritizes structural geometry, legibility in low ambient light, and distinct numerical clarity. Plus Jakarta Sans handles both humanistic text readability and mechanical metric presentation.

Rules for application:
- **Timer & Numerical Displays**: Use `timer-numeric` and tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time countdown updates.
- **Headlines**: Render with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to impart intentional typographic polish.
- **Micro-labels**: Apply `label-caps` in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) on status indicators, gauge legends, and session metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric bento grid that scales from single-column mobile viewports to modular multi-pane desktop consoles:

- **Mobile (Base)**: Single-column stack governed by a `1.25rem` outer canvas margin. Bento cards stretch fluidly with `space-md` (`1rem`) vertical separation.
- **Tablet & Split-Panels (>= 768px)**: 4-to-6 column fluid arrangement. Hero timer dials occupy primary 3-column slots, paired with stacked utility modules (audio waves, task checklist, session heatmap).
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column bento architecture with `gutter-desktop` (`1.5rem`) and `margin-desktop` (`2.5rem`), supporting side-by-side gauge monitoring and persistent timeline control.
- **Internal Density**: Component padding strictly adheres to `space-md` for standard cards and `space-lg` for prominent rotary dial containers.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses soft, tactile, high-radius contours that invite touch:

- **Cards & Modules**: Rounded with `28px` to `32px` corners (`rounded-3xl`), conveying an organic, handheld industrial quality.
- **Action Buttons & Capsules**: Fully rounded pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for segmented tabs, play heads, and session triggers.
- **Notches & Gauges**: Circular analog time rings (`rounded-full`) embedded with radial line markers and rounded mechanical progress needles.

## Components

### Buttons & Capsule Triggers
- **Primary Pill**: Height `52px`, terracotta background (`#E75A44`), bold text in `#FFFFFF`, fully rounded. On tap: subtle scaling (`scale(0.98)`) with intensified warm glow.
- **Ghost Utility Button**: Height `40px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, housing crisp monoline icons.

### Analog-Digital Focus Dial
- High-contrast ceramic cream face (`#EADECF`) or obsidian gauge ring.
- Radial tick notches spaced at 5-minute increments with a sweeping burnt vermilion arc fill.
- Tactile center cap with dual concentric bevel rings and mechanical needle indicator.

### Segmented Session Controllers
- Recessed pill trough in `#0D0E10`.
- Sliding active indicator in `#25282E` with a hairline highlight border.
- Text labels transition from `#7E838D` (inactive) to `#F6F6F7` (selected).

### Cards & Bento Modules
- Surface in `#1C1E22`, radius `28px`, padding `space-lg`.
- Top header row contains uppercase tracking labels (`label-caps`) paired with minimalist ellipsis or icon actions.
- Content zones support nested micro-components such as audio wave visualizers, calendar day selectors, and session intensity grids.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Recessed background `#16181B` with smooth `16px` curvature.
- Focus ring: `1px solid #E75A44` alongside `0 0 0 3px rgba(231, 90, 68, 0.15)`.

### Audio / Lo-Fi Player Widget
- Horizontal capsule layout featuring dynamic vertical waveform bars.
- Live bars shift dynamically between muted slate and terracotta glow.
- Pill-shaped play/pause counterweight with integrated track labels.
