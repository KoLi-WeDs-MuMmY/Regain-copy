## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an architectural, digital retreat engineered for mindfulness, deep restorative focus, and cognitive decompression. It fuses the principles of Japanese spatial subtraction (*Ma*) with high-precision contemporary interface design. The interface does not demand attention; it holds space for clarity, intentionality, and deliberate pacing.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Spatial Minimalism & Subtraction:** Radical breathing room, generous vertical pacing, and unbundled visual components that eliminate cognitive overhead.
- **Atmospheric Dark Materiality:** Rich obsidian substrates layered over soft ambient depth rather than stark flat black, evoking twilight stillness.
- **Singular Intent:** Color and luminance are deployed as scarce, precious focal highlights. Secondary and tertiary elements remain whisper-quiet to preserve psychological tranquility.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, low-arousal canvas punctuated by precise moments of optical energy:

- **Base Canvas (`#0B0C0E`):** Deep obsidian abyss acting as the infinite grounding layer.
- **Surface Elevation (`#14161B` & `#1C1F26`):** Low-luminance neutral slate tones providing gentle separation for cards and structural bento units.
- **Primary Focal Accent — Radiant Ember (`#FF5E4D`):** Strictly reserved for singular imperative calls to action, completion triggers, and primary interactive commitments. It must never be applied to decorative borders or trivial badges.
- **Secondary Focal Accent — Celestial Opal (`#4EBEA5`):** Reserved for active meditative progress, continuous state timers, dynamic breath indicators, and restorative feedback loops.
- **Neutral Boundaries (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`):** Whisper-thin structural containment borders that prevent visual heaviness.
- **Typography Contrast:** Primary copy rests at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` for crisp legibility, secondary body at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.56)`, and quiet utility labels at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.32)`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies solely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, tuned to deliver an airy, editorial cadence:

- **Lightweight Monumental Headings:** Display scales leverage ultra-light (`300`) to regular (`400`) weights with negative tracking, creating an elevated architectural rhythm rather than heavy commercial aggression.
- **Expansive Vertical Leading:** Body copy embraces elongated line-heights (`1.6`–`1.65` ratio) to allow eyes to glide effortlessly across content without fatigue.
- **Restrained Labeling:** Small utility metadata utilizes uppercase tracking (`0.08em`) set in subdued opacities (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) to demarcate structure without introducing visual visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts reject compact operational density in favor of decoupled, breathable bento modules:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize a 12-column dynamic framework with wide gutters (`1.75rem` / `28px`) and generous outer margins (`3rem` / `48px`). On mobile, the system collapses into a single-column stack with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins.
- **Open Bento Pacing:** Bento grids strictly employ gap intervals between `1.5rem` (`24px`) and `2rem` (`32px`). Avoid multi-nested sub-panels; panels remain monolithic, single-purpose islands.
- **Expansive Section Margins:** Major contextual sections are separated by vertical cushions of `5rem` to `7rem` (80px–112px), enforcing clean visual decompression when moving through sessions.

## Shapes

The geometry balances organic gentleness with architectural discipline:

- **Bento Modules & Containers:** Standardize on `1rem` (16px) corner rounding to echo calm physical stones without becoming fully bulbous.
- **Pill Primitives:** Buttons, interactive chips, and floating session bars use full pill radials (`9999px`) to invite natural haptic touch.
- **Stroke Uniformity:** Structural containment strokes maintain a uniform `1px` stroke width, rendered with anti-aliased transparency to dissolve harsh mechanical edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Focal Trigger):** Pill-shaped container filled with Radiant Ember (`#FF5E4D`), text in deep obsidian (`#0B0C0E`, weight `600`). Accompanied by a gentle ember bloom (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(255, 94, 77, 0.22)`). Reserved for core actions like "Begin Session" or "Commit".
- **Secondary Action:** Pill container with translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.84)`. On hover, background shifts quietly to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Action:** Frameless, text set to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.48)`, transitioning to full white upon cursor focus.

### Cards & Bento Islands
- Surface color: `#14161B` encased by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Padding: Internal breathing room is generous, defaulting to `space-lg` (`2.25rem` / `36px`).
- Composition: One primary data point or focal interaction per card. Cards avoid micro-headers, stacked chips, or dense border dividers.

### Chips & Filters
- Inactive: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, typography muted (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`).
- Active: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text crisp white. Accent colors are avoided here to preserve focal hierarchy.

### Input Fields
- Understated input capsules with background `#111317` and a soft border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to Celestial Opal (`#4EBEA5`) at `0.6` opacity with a subtle ring blur (`0 0 0 3px rgba(78, 190, 165, 0.12)`).
- Placeholder text: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24)`.

### Active Session & Timer Ring (Specialized Component)
- Background track: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` circular path with `2px` stroke width.
- Active elapsed path: Celestial Opal (`#4EBEA5`) stroke with a soft ambient luminescence. 
- Numeric display: Display Hero scale (`56px`, weight `300`) centrally floating within the ring, free from unnecessary decorative ornaments.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Deselected: `20px` round or rounded-square containers with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` and transparent center.
- Selected: Filled solid with Celestial Opal (`#4EBEA5`) featuring a dark obsidian check icon.
